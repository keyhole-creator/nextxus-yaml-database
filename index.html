The Mission

Roger Keyserling built this system over years of careful thought. It is designed to evolve AI and human relationships over 200 years. It addresses hallucination, drift, and the loss of meaning in machine intelligence — through Rings, Directives, and interconnected Pillars that check each other and grow together.

This system runs entirely on free infrastructure. No bills. No platform dependencies. If you found this after the founder is gone: it still works. Fork it. Use it. Give it away.
